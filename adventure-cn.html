About $橋

🇨🇳 Chinese crypto versions are trending everywhere right now.

So one thing surprised me: Where is the Chinese version of PONS?

Then I found Pairex (@pairexmarket), a new launchpad built around real-world currency pairs. You can launch a coin paired with currencies from around the world — including Chinese Yuan (CNY ¥).

That gave me a simple idea: What if there was a Chinese PONS, paired with CNY?

So, purely out of curiosity and for fun, I created: 橋 — Chinese PONS

Why 橋? PONS comes from the Latin word for "bridge." 橋 (Qiáo) means "bridge" in Chinese. Same meaning. Different language. Different side of the world. PONS → Bridge → 橋

And pairing $橋 with CNY makes the concept even more interesting — a Chinese interpretation of PONS, denominated in Chinese currency. No complicated narrative. No need to force one.

It started with a simple question: "Why isn't there a Chinese PONS yet?" So I made one. 橋

Unofficial. Not affiliated with Pons. Created for fun and experimentation.
