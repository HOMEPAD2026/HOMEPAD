# ARCIRCLE WORLD × Arc: partnership one-pager (draft for the owner)

## What ARCIRCLE WORLD is
A browser 3D game world built on Arc. It's one connected town with 8+ games inside: Jurassic survival, SURVIVORS ARENA,
a poker lounge, INFINITY RINGS, RAID QUEENS, a mine, tower defence and more. Players also make their own games in
ARCIRCLE STUDIO (Beta) and publish them to the web at arcircle.app/game/@creator. It plays on phones and computers,
with no download.

## What already runs on Arc (built and tested)
- **USDC from any chain into the game.** Players bring USDC from 14 CCTP chains straight to their Arc wallet inside the
  game. It's burned on the source chain and minted on Arc by Circle's Forwarding Service, so the player needs no gas on Arc.
- **USDC payments in the game.** Mythic items trade for USDC. The buyer's transfer is checked on Arc, and the team pays
  sellers from its own wallet. The server never holds a key.
- **Crimson Celestial NFTs on Arc.** An ERC-721 with EIP-712 vouchers, deployed at
  0x709E1B65c529567Fbe1C79dE278c2ABbA0086ff5. A wallet-to-wallet USDC market contract (CrimsonMarket) is written and
  tested, and is ready to deploy.
- **$ARCIRCLE on Arc.** It's used for AI agents (AGENT DIMENSION), LAND and dungeon tickets. Half of those payments is burned.
- **Creator economy.** A weekly Studio contest with a USDC prize for the top 3, paid on Arc and checked by the server.

## Why it matters for Arc
- Every new player gets an Arc wallet and their first USDC on Arc through a game, not through a trading app.
- It's a showcase of App Kits / CCTP in a consumer product that people use for fun every day.
- Our 2026 targets: 10,000+ registered players, 100+ at the same time, 10+ playable games.

## What we'd like to do together
1. **Arc Studio + ARCIRCLE STUDIO.** Players' game ideas turned into on-chain mini-games (prize pools, tickets) with Arc
   Studio as the builder behind the scenes.
2. **App Kit Swap and Unified Balance** inside the game: pay with any stablecoin, one balance across chains. This needs
   a Circle API key on our server.
3. **Co-marketing.** A "Built on Arc" badge in the game and on /game, joint tournaments with USDC prizes, and an Arc
   ecosystem listing.
4. **Grants / ecosystem support** for the 100 days of building.
