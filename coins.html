What each coin is

$ARCIRCLEToken on Arc

The core token of ARCIRCLE — an ERC-20 on Arc mainnet. You hold it in your own wallet; in the game it pays for items and LAND, and it settles the exchange.

  • ChainArc mainnet (chain id 5042)
  • Supply1,000,000,000 minted once — the contract has no mint function
  • BurningCoins sent to 0x…dEaD are gone for good; every burn is on the chain
  • Contract0xe5718F298ac3b65FAf7c711b56cBD72b3bb15fF7View on ArcScan
ARCLIn-game coin

The game's own coin. You earn it by playing and spend it in the world. It isn't launched as a token yet — today ARCL are in-game points, kept by the game's server.

  • Earn8 games, the town, quests, your island
  • SpendShop, forge, pickaxes, poker chips, the Creator Market
  • ExchangeARCL ⇄ $ARCIRCLE by request in the game; a GM settles each one
  • NextA token launch is planned (on Solana or Robinhood Chain)

🔥 Planned $ARCL token fee: 2% a transaction — 1% buys back and burns $ARCIRCLE, 1% funds ARCIRCLE WORLD's development. Today's in-game ARCL has no fee.
