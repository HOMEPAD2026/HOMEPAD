How claiming works

1
Pick an open capital.

First come, first served — paired with $HOMEPAD, no ETH needed to launch.

2
Confirm the launch in your wallet.

Name, ticker and logo are fixed by the map — you can't change them, which is what makes a claim provable.

3
Defend it, any time.

After the first 5 minutes, stay at or above $50K market cap and it's yours. Drop below that — even years later — and anyone can reset the capital by paying 2,000,000 $HOMEPAD to the $HOME treasury, then launch it themselves.

4
You're paid the whole time.

70% of every trade's fee goes to your wallet inside the trade, 30% to $HOME. Liquidity is locked forever, no admin key. The map reads the chain, not a database — resets included.
