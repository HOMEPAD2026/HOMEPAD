🗺️ What is this, actually?

Picture a map of the entire world. On it, every country's capital city — Seoul, Washington D.C., Tokyo, Paris, all 197 of them — has exactly one coin tied to it. Not a coin representing the country in some abstract way — a real, tradeable coin, launched through HOMEPAD's normal launch system, that happens to carry that capital's name.

There is only ever one coin per capital. Once someone launches "Seoul," there is no second Seoul — ever. The rule is permanent and enforced by the smart contracts themselves, not by anyone's promise.

👆 How to claim a capital, step by step

1
Find an open capital. On the map, capitals with a small grey ring are still unclaimed. The list below the map shows every one, with a search box if you know which country you want.
2
Tap it. A window pops up showing exactly what you'd be launching: the coin's name (the country), its ticker (the capital), and its logo (that country's own outline map) — all locked in, not editable.
3
Connect your wallet and confirm. This is one normal transaction — the same kind of transaction as launching any coin on HOMEPAD. No ETH is required to launch; it's paired with $HOMEPAD instead.
4
Done. You are now the coin's creator, permanently, on-chain. The capital lights up on the map immediately.

Why can't you pick your own name or ticker? Because that's exactly what makes a claim provable. If anyone could type any name, there'd be no way to tell a "real" Seoul from a fake one. Locking it to the map's own rule means anyone, at any time, can independently check whether a claim is legitimate — including you, right now, on the "Recent activity" list further down this page.

💸 What you actually earn

Every single trade on your coin — someone buying it, someone selling it — automatically pays a small 1% fee, split two ways the instant the trade happens:

  • 70% comes straight to your wallet. Not a balance you have to claim later. Not something sitting in a contract waiting for you to withdraw it. It arrives with the trade, in the same transaction.
  • 30% goes to $HOME, HOMEPAD's own coin — this is what funds the whole platform.

You don't need to do anything to receive this. No staking, no dashboard to check in on, no button to press. It just happens, for as long as you hold the capital.

🛡️ Defending your capital

Owning a capital isn't a one-time win — it's something you have to keep earning, in a very specific, very fair way: real demand. Here's exactly how that's measured:

🌱 First 5 minutesCompletely safe, no matter what. This is just enough time for your own launch transaction to fully settle — nobody can snipe you the instant you claim.
🔒 $50,000+ market capFully protected, for as long as you stay above it. A day, a month, a year — doesn't matter. Nobody can touch it.
↻ Under $50,000(after those first 5 minutes) Anyone can take it from you — see below for exactly how.

Think of it like a house that has to stay lived-in. As long as people actually care about your coin enough to trade it up past $50K, it's yours, permanently. If it goes quiet and the value drops, the door is technically unlocked for someone more motivated to move in.

↻ How someone takes a capital from you

This isn't a hack, a hidden exploit, or anything sneaky — it's a plain, published rule anyone can use, including you, against someone else's weak capital:

1
Find a capital marked "resettable" (a pulsing red marker on the map, or a red tag in the list).
2
Pay 2,000,000 $HOMEPAD to the $HOME treasury — a real, verifiable payment, not a promise.
3
Launch that capital yourself, right after. You're now the creator — the old coin keeps trading on its own, untouched, but the map, the name, and every future fee now point to you.

The app checks this on-chain before it ever shows a takeover as valid: it looks for your $HOMEPAD payment landing after the previous owner's 5-minute grace period ended, and before your new launch. There's no way to fake this from the outside.

🔥 $NHOOD mottos — giving your capital personality

This part is entirely optional and purely cosmetic. If you currently hold a capital, you can burn 10,000 $NHOOD (HOMEPAD's companion token for this game, either its Pons or Pairex version) to inscribe a short custom line next to your flag — something like 🇰🇷 South Korea『Home of $HOME』. It shows up everywhere your capital's name appears: the list, the activity feed, the map. It never touches the coin's real name, ticker, or contract — it's a flourish, not a feature that changes anything economically.

🔍 Why you don't have to trust anyone here

There is no admin panel, no team member, and no database quietly deciding who "really" owns Seoul. Every single claim and every single reset is read directly from the blockchain, live, every time this page loads. The "Recent activity" feed further down shows the actual on-chain events with a link to the real transaction for each one — you can click through and verify every claim on this entire map yourself, using nothing but a public block explorer.
