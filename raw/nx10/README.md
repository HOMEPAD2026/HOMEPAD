# raw/nx10 — UI kit: buttons, icons, headers, glow FX (Meshy text-to-image, raw files)

Credits: 81 (27 images at 3 each: 26 plus one redo). Balance 1973 → 1892.
No logos, brand marks or readable text in any picture.

## buttons/ — made at 1024 on lime green, cropped to the button with an even 8 % margin, then scaled to 512 wide
Front view, empty, with a soft shadow; the pills are 9-slice-able (round ends, even middle). Key the green by hue.
| file | what |
|---|---|
| btn-primary | cyan→mint gradient glass pill with a bright rim (its surrounding green is a little bluer than the others) |
| btn-gold | polished gold pill |
| btn-glass | dark navy glass pill with a thin glowing cyan edge |
| btn-danger | coral-red glass pill |
| btn-round | round button: solid navy glass disc, glowing cyan rim, glossy highlight. Redone: the first had a see-through centre that let the green show, so keying would have left only a ring |
| tab-on | lit cyan/mint glass rounded tab |
| tab-off | dim navy glass rounded tab |

## icons/ — 512², on lime green (key by hue), a single glossy object each
bridge (light arch with a coin between two islands), chain-link, wallet (blue glass with a gold coin), usdc-coin (a plain
blue coin with a white $ and a white rim, no Circle logo), prize-trophy (gold cup with a cyan gem), payout (blue coin
stack with an up arrow), market-nft (crimson crystal card on a gold stand), tutorial-cap (graduation cap with a cyan
tassel on a small smiling glass cloud), arrow-guide (gold arrow outline with a cyan glass fill), check-badge (mint check
in a glass circle), lock (glass padlock with a gold shackle), clock (glass stopwatch with gold buttons).

## headers/ — 1344×768, subject on the right, calm space middle-left for a title
| file | what |
|---|---|
| hdr-bridge | two floating islands joined by a glowing coin path, ARON (halo) waving on the right island, blue sky |
| hdr-nftmarket | crimson-and-gold showroom with crystal plinths, glowing crimson armour, helmet, shield and blade (the shield carries a sun-face emblem; not a brand) |
| hdr-prize | gold trophy on a stage under spotlights with confetti; a white-and-blue glass city soft behind. The trophy has a small blank dark plate |
| hdr-tutorial | ARON in a graduation cap, large in the right foreground, pointing down a glowing cyan path to a tall glass tower |

## fx/ — 512², pure black background, for additive blending
coin-trail (a wavy line of glowing blue coins), success-burst (mint radial star burst), bridge-beam (horizontal cyan beam with sparkles).
