Raw text-to-image results (`node tools/meshy.mjs image "<prompt>" --ratio <r>`), 2026-10-28. Not packed yet:
convert to WebP and size for a phone before they go on the page. `empty.png` is on a flat green to be keyed out by hue
(the green came out lighter than #00ff00, but even).

> Bright stylized 3D render, cute small white robot hero with a black visor face, glowing cyan eyes and a thin cyan halo

> with cyan and mint glow accents and a little warm gold, clean composition. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no
> watermark, no brands.

and the banners (learn, store, community, rewards, analytics, updates) also carry:

> The left third of the frame is kept darker and empty for a headline; the subject sits in the right two thirds.
