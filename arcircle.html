What $ARCIRCLE is

One coin at the center of two launchpads.

The flywheel

Every launch turns the wheel. The wheel turns for $ARCIRCLE.

More launches mean more revenue. More revenue means more going back into $ARCIRCLE. A stronger $ARCIRCLE brings in more creators — and the loop starts again.

  1. 1

    Launch

    Creators launch coins on ArcPad and raise together on CirclePad. Every launch brings new people and new volume to Arc.

  2. 2

    Earn

    Launch fees, ArcPad's platform allocation and trading fees, CirclePad's raise share, and $ARCIRCLE's own creator fee flow in.

  3. 3

    Buy back

    That revenue goes back into $ARCIRCLE — buybacks and liquidity support that work for its value.

  4. 4

    Grow

    A stronger $ARCIRCLE funds holder and creator rewards (coming soon) and draws in more creators — so the next round of launches is bigger.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

What is the contract address?

What is the contract address?

There isn't one yet — $ARCIRCLE is relaunching. The new contract address will be posted on this page and by @ARCIRCLEonArc when it goes live. Don't trust addresses from anywhere else.

Where can I buy it?

Where can I buy it?

On Argus, where it launched. It trades in a Uniswap v4 pool on Arc, so it also shows up on Dexscreener and other trackers that index Arc — always check the contract address first.

Has any $ARCIRCLE been burned?

Yes. On 25 September 2026, 116,772,290.56 $ARCIRCLE — 11.68% of the total supply — was sent to the dead address 0x000…dEaD, which no one controls. Those tokens can never move again; the live total is shown above. See the transaction ↗

Is the liquidity locked?

Yes. Argus puts the whole supply into a single Uniswap v4 position when the pool is created and hands it to a locker contract that has no withdraw function — nobody, Argus included, can pull that liquidity.

What happens at graduation?

When the real USDC in the curve reaches its graduation threshold, trading moves from the curve to a DEX pool with that liquidity. The progress above is read live from the curve.

Is there a team allocation?

No. Argus put all 1,000,000,000 $ARCIRCLE into the pool's liquidity position at launch; there is no separate team allocation. The supply split above shows where it sits today.

Is there a team allocation?

No. $ARCIRCLE launched with 100% of its 1,000,000,000 supply in the public curve. The supply split above shows where it sits today.

What is the creator fee?

Every $ARCIRCLE trade pays the launch's buy or sell tax — set once at launch between 1% and 10%, and fixed forever — on top of the 1% pool fee. Argus keeps 10% of the tax; the other 90% goes to the creator's allocation, in USDC. That share is one of the five revenue sources that feed the flywheel.

Do holders get rewards?

A reward program for holders and creators is in design, funded by ecosystem revenue — never by new tokens. Nothing is promised until its rules are published on the Reward page.
