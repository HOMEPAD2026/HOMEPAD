Most places that let you launch a coin make you pick a lane before you understand what any of the lanes mean. HOMEPAD keeps it simple: there are three doors in, and every single one of them leads to the same place — a real token, live and tradeable, the moment you walk through.

Pick a door. Add a name, a picture, a bit of a story. Walk out the other side with a coin that actually exists.

None of these doors need you to know what a smart contract is. You fill in a name, add a picture if you've got one, and pick the door that matches what you're building. That's the whole decision.
