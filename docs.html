FAQ

Is this affiliated with Robinhood?

No. HOMEPAD is an independent, permissionless application built on Robinhood Chain. Robinhood Markets does not operate, review, or endorse it.

What are the launch modes?

Three ETH-paired modes — Hybrid (default, live — a real pool from block one, no ETH needed to launch), Bonding curve (soon — graduates into a pool once it raises enough), and Instant liquidity (live — a real two-sided pool you seed with ETH yourself) — plus Stock pair (soon), which is Hybrid priced in a Robinhood Stock Token instead of ETH. See the mechanism page for how each actually works.

Where do fees go?

Every trade pays a 1% fee, split 70% to the token's creator and 30% to the $HOME treasury — the platform wallet takes 0% at the deployed settings. A creator can add up to 2% extra at launch, which is all theirs. The fee comes out of whatever the swap pays out: on a buy it's taken in the token being bought, on a sell it's taken in ETH. Separately, 8% of every launch's supply goes to the $HOME treasury at creation (a one-time allocation, not a fee) — it's burned by hand until the lock contract ships. See the mechanism page for the details.

I launched a coin — what exactly do I receive?

70% of every trade's fee, paid straight to your wallet the moment the trade happens. It arrives in whatever the trade paid out: when someone buys your coin you receive your coin; when someone sells it you receive ETH. Nothing to claim, nothing held by anyone. Your Profile shows the running total, with the token-denominated part valued in ETH at each trade's price.

Can a launch be paused or rugged by HOMEPAD itself?

No admin key exists in these contracts. Nobody — including whoever runs HOMEPAD — can pause, drain, or redirect a launch once it's live.

Is HOMEPAD live yet?

Yes — HOMEPAD is live on Robinhood Chain mainnet. Hybrid and Instant Liquidity launches are real; Bonding Curve and Stock Pair are still coming soon.
