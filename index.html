$HOMEPAD V2 coming.

HOMEPAD will soon transition to V2 with a newly issued token. Existing holders will receive the corresponding amount of HOMEPAD V2 based on the value of their V1 holdings at the snapshot.

The purpose of V2 is bigger than simply issuing a new token. The core launchpad infrastructure is being rebuilt around PONS, with the goal of a more established and security-focused foundation for the next stage of HOMEPAD.

Important for V1 holders
  • Keep holding your V1 tokens. If you sell after the snapshot, you will not be eligible for the V2 distribution.
  • The snapshot has already been taken. V1 purchases made after the snapshot will not increase your V2 allocation.
  • Your V2 allocation is based solely on your holdings recorded at the snapshot.
Hold V1 → Receive V2. 🏡

Details on V2, the migration process, and the new launchpad structure will be announced separately. More coming soon.
