How rent gets home

1
Every trade pays 1% rent.

70% to the coin's creator, 30% to the $HOME treasury — inside the trade itself. A buy pays it in the coin being bought; a sell pays it in ETH. Creators receive theirs the same way.

2
Every launch sends 8% of supply to the treasury.

A one-time allocation baked into the launch — the same idea as Pons V2 — meant to support the ecosystem and fund continuous liquidity as HOMEPAD grows.

3
The treasury burns it. Then it locks it.

Today that's a manual burn, with every tx listed above. The contract that will hold it locked and turn treasury assets into liquidity for launches is in development.
