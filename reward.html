Design principles

Four rules the program is built on.

Funded by revenue

Rewards come out of real ecosystem income. No new $ARCIRCLE is ever created to pay them.

Hard to farm

Unannounced snapshots, minimum holding periods and activity measured over windows make short-term balances and wash trading expensive.

Verifiable

Eligibility rules are published, and anyone can recompute the results from public chain data.

Simple to claim

One place to see eligibility and claim — this page, once the program is live.

Under study

Candidate mechanics.

These are the designs being evaluated now. The final program may use some, all or none of them.

FAQ

Questions about rewards.

Are rewards live?

Not yet. Burns are live today in every utility; the reward contract and the holder and creator programs are being built, and they only go live once their rules are published on this page.

What is the burn engine?

A reward contract that receives utility and platform revenue and buys back and burns $ARCIRCLE and $ARCIA automatically, so no one has to press a button. The core framework is being built; contract details, burn ratios and the rollout timeline are shared as each piece is ready.

How are burns labeled by source?

Each transfer to 0x…dEaD is matched to its transaction: the contract it called (burn-to-vote, Builder Mine, ARCIA DESK), or the Token Scanner unlock or secret file it paid for. Anything else is shown as a direct burn from a wallet.

Do I need to do anything now?

No. There is nothing to register or claim today. The wallet check above only shows the on-chain activity the programs are designed around.

Will buying just before a snapshot help?

The candidate design uses time-weighted balances and unannounced snapshots, so a balance held for a moment counts for very little.

Where does the money come from?

From what the ecosystem earns: the five revenue sources above. No new $ARCIRCLE is ever minted to pay rewards.

Does my vote in the poll decide the design?

It is a signal, not a binding vote. It shows which mechanics the community cares about while the rules are being written.

Is holding $ARCIRCLE a claim on revenue?

No. $ARCIRCLE is a community memecoin — holding it is not a share, a claim on revenue or a right to any payment.
