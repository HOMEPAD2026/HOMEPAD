Design principles

Four rules the program is built on.

Funded by revenue

Rewards come out of real ecosystem income. No new $ARCIRCLE is ever created to pay them.

Hard to farm

Unannounced snapshots, minimum holding periods and activity measured over windows make short-term balances and wash trading expensive.

Verifiable

Eligibility rules are published, and anyone can recompute the results from public chain data.

Simple to claim

One place to see eligibility and claim — this page, once the program is live.

Under study

Candidate mechanics.

These are the designs being evaluated now. The final program may use some, all or none of them.

Holders

Time-weighted snapshots

Your average balance over an epoch counts — not the balance in a single block. Buying just before a snapshot doesn't help.

Creators

Creator score

Combines a coin's organic volume, unique buyers and holder retention, with a boost for launches paired with $ARCIRCLE.

Everyone

Referral share

A slice of fees for wallets and communities that bring in launches, once referral support is added to the routers.

Roadmap

Where rewards sit on the roadmap.

  1. Phase 0 — Foundation

    Q3 2026 · Done

    $ARCIRCLE launched with 100% of supply in its public curve. ArcPad live on Arc mainnet. CirclePad escrow deployed.

  2. Phase 1 — First rounds

    Q4 2026 · In progress

    Publish the flywheel revenue split. First treasury buybacks of $ARCIRCLE, posted with transaction links. First CirclePad round.

  3. Phase 2 — Rewards

    Q4 2026 – Q1 2027 · Planned
    • Holder rewards v1 — time-weighted snapshots, funded by revenue
    • Creator rewards v1 — based on organic volume, buyers and retention
    • Rewards dashboard and claim on this page
    • Referral support in the routers, with a fee share for referrers

  4. Phase 3 — CirclePad v2

    Q1 – Q2 2027 · Planned

    Lead vesting with automatic clawback, and the CirclePad participant airdrop — eligibility published before anything goes live.
