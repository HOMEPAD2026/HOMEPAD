rent goes home.

All that rent doesn't sit in a vault somewhere. It goes home — bought straight back into $HOME, off the open market.

This is the part that makes the whole thing worth doing. Every coin launched, every trade made, sends a little bit of rent home — 30% of every trade fee lands in the $HOME treasury, on-chain, the moment the trade happens (the other 70% goes to the coin's creator). "Home" means $HOME itself gets bought back from the market with it, by nothing more than people using HOMEPAD the normal way.

Every coin launched here quietly makes the shared home a little stronger.
