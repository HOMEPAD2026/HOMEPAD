ARCIRCLE Orders

Open ARCIRCLE Orders
v8
  • One cancels other: a limit and a stop on the same amount — buy the dip or the breakout, or sell into a rise or cut on a fall. The first to fill cancels the other on-chain.
  • Buy with take-profit and stop-loss: three orders signed at once. The two exits wait for the buy and arm the moment it fills; the first of them to fill cancels the other.
  • Pause and resume: take an order — or every order in a market — out of the book without cancelling it. One signature, no gas; when the Token Scanner flags a coin you hold orders in, a banner offers to pause them.
  • Why it hasn't filled: each open order says what it waits for — the price gap, a thin pool, funding, a pause or the order before it. Unfunded orders get a Fix button that restores the approval or balance they need.
  • Solana gets the full set: stop, TP / SL, trailing stop, timed buys (DCA), and for Pump.fun coins At graduation and Curve mark. You sign the condition as a plain message (no fee); the keeper waits for it, and the program's worst-price floor still holds.
  • A weekly season: the volume your orders filled this week, Monday to Monday UTC, on a board in Stats. Just for fun — no rewards are set.
  • For bots: a read-only API key (it can read your open orders and fills, never place or cancel) and a live stream of your own order events.
  • Notes on your orders (kept in this browser), a cancel you can undo for 5 seconds, copy any trade from the tape into the form, the network fee before you place, layout presets (chart first, balanced, book first), and Solana in the portfolio.
  • On a phone: swipe an order right to edit it, left to cancel, or hold it for the menu. Chart labels that sit close together stack into one, and the price glows when it comes within 3% of an order.
v7
  • Grid orders: buys at steps under the pool price (2, 3, 5 or 10% apart, 3 to 8 levels), each with a sell one step higher that waits for its own buy to fill. Two signatures a level, no gas; a sell joins the book only once its buy has filled.
  • Fill odds: under the price, how often the pool reached it within a day in its own candles of the last few days, and when it last did. A record of the past, not a forecast. The odds also follow an order line while you drag it on the chart.
  • A wider desk on big screens: the chart takes the room, and the edges between the book, the chart and the form can be dragged (double-click resets them). F puts the book and the chart over the whole screen; Esc leaves.
  • Move all by any percent you type, or only your sells or only your buys. Ask ARCIA "move my sells +5%" and the move is set up for you to check and sign.
  • A condition can watch the other chain: $ARCIRCLE on Arc from Robinhood Chain, $ARCIA on Robinhood Chain from Arc.
  • Price alerts are kept for your wallet by default, so the ARCIA bot (/orderalerts on) and browser notifications reach you with the tab closed, on any device. A watchlist tab keeps your starred markets, with alerts on all of them at once.
  • Portfolio across both chains, in dollars. The journal adds the last 7 and 30 days, the best and worst sell, the latest sells, a share image and a CSV. A filled order shows how much better than its limit it filled.
  • Explore sorts by volume, 24h change, liquidity or age, and can hide coins under a day old. Stats adds the most-filled markets, the $ARCIRCLE burned by day, and the executor's recent runs, gas and address.
  • ARCIA's morning note on Telegram (with /orderalerts on): what filled in the last 24 hours, what's open, and the order the price is closest to.
  • For bots: a public fill feed and stream, a signed webhook for your own wallet's events, and a small client (/sdk/arcircle-orders/).
  • The phone's order sheet opens at half height: swipe up for all of it, down to go back. The place button stays in view on a desktop, the explainer folds once you know a type, and fills land with a stamp and a flame to the burn counter (sound and vibration can be turned off).

ARCIRCLE Predict

Open ARCIRCLE Predict
v3
  • The round card opens with a Bet → Live → Settle band and a gauge: its needle shows how far the price is from the price to beat, with a bar for the gap. Your stake is marked on the pool bar, a coin drops into a side when a bet lands, the payout multiples roll as they change, and the "is winning" pill flips over when the lead changes hands.
  • Under the pools, a small chart shows how the UP / DOWN split moved as bets came in. A round with no bets yet says it plainly: the first bet sets the odds, and with nobody on the other side everyone gets their stake back.
  • Practice mode: bet play money on the real rounds ($100 on Arc, 0.05 ETH on Robinhood Chain), settled on the real result. Nothing leaves your wallet; with nobody on the other side, practice pays an even pot.
  • Alerts follow you: with a wallet connected, the bell also sends your rounds' results to this device with the tab closed, and on Telegram, /predictalerts 0x… (add rh for Robinhood Chain) DMs you wins, losses, refunds, a side losing the lead and winnings still unclaimed after 6 hours.
  • "Your bets" collects what you can claim on both chains — the other chain is one tap (switch, then claim). Your PnL comes with its last 30 days as a line, and "Share my stats card" posts a card with your win rate, PnL, best streak and weekly podiums.
  • Last rounds opens with a heat map of the market's last 50 results. Live bets keeps the last 30 bets even in a quiet hour.
  • The leaderboard shows the top 3 on a podium, each wallet's fan tier (from the $ARCIA it holds on Robinhood Chain) and its weekly podiums. Every finished week keeps its top 3 under Past seasons.
  • Tell ARCIA a bet in her chat — "UP $2 on ARCIRCLE 5m", "down 1 usdc arcircle 15m", "up 0.001 eth arcia on rh" — and she opens Predict with the market, side and amount filled in. You check it and sign; nothing is placed before that.
  • The wallet that lists a market can give it an end date (1, 7 or 30 days, a signature, no gas); the keeper stops it then. On Robinhood Chain anyone can list a graduated Pons coin when the team opens listing. The keeper also keeps $ARCIA markets open on both chains (5m, 15m and 1h) — on Robinhood Chain once $ARCIA graduates.
  • The keeper chip turns amber when the keeper is 5 minutes late or reports a problem, and the page keeps trying when a chain doesn't answer instead of going blank.
